# NPSP Individual Giving

Skills for individual giving fundraisers at nonprofits using Salesforce Nonprofit Success Pack (NPSP).

Department: Resource Development
Persona: Individual Giving Fundraiser

## Skills

| Skill | Use it when | Output |
|---|---|---|
| donor-meeting-prep | You have a donor meeting coming up | One-page donor brief with giving summary, talking points, and suggested ask |
| donor-meeting-follow-up | You just met with a donor | Contact report, follow-up email, NPSP update checklist |
| donor-thank-you-notes | New gifts came in | Tiered, personalized thank-you drafts and acknowledgment updates |
| monthly-pipeline-review | It's time for your monthly pipeline check | Pipeline summary, stalled list, priority actions |
| gift-processing | You have checks, an online payout, or a special gift to enter | Step-by-step entry guide, coding checks, finance batch summary |

## How data gets in

These skills work without a Salesforce connection. Paste data or upload an NPSP report export. If a Salesforce connector is connected, Claude can read records directly.

## Defaults

Each skill ships with defaults (for example, thank donors within 48 hours; flag gifts over $5,000). Tell Claude your org's standard and it will use that instead. Claude asks for anything that varies too much to assume, such as your Campaign and GAU list or your annual goal.

## Works alongside

The Claude for Nonprofits plugin's donor-and-funder-relations skill covers receipt language and donor privacy. These skills defer to it when both are installed.
